Privacy Policy

Information about data associated with your use of SimTrail.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

About SimTrail

SimTrail is a companion app for exploring virtual aviation traffic, flights, airports and air traffic control information from the VATSIM network.

Network data

The app requests flight, airport and controller information from external network services to present its features. Requests to these services may expose technical connection information, such as an IP address, to the service operators. Their handling of this information is governed by their own policies.

Information you choose to share

If you submit a bug report or support request through GitHub Issues, the information you provide is processed by GitHub. Public issues are visible to others. Do not include passwords or confidential data in support requests.

Device and app data

Some app features may store preferences or flight-related information on your device. The exact information used depends on the features enabled and the version of SimTrail. You can remove locally stored app information using the controls available on your device, although some data may require separate deletion if the app uses synchronization or backups.

Third-party services

SimTrail relies on network services to display VATSIM-related information. For more information about these services, see VATSIM and GitHub's privacy statement.

Children's privacy

SimTrail is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted through a support request, please contact support.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, visit the SimTrail support page or open a GitHub support request.
