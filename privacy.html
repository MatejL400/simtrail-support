Privacy Policy

Your view of the virtual sky.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

SimTrail is an independent companion application for viewing virtual air traffic, air traffic control activity, airports, flight information, and selected aviation weather data. This Privacy Policy explains how SimTrail handles information when you use the app.

SimTrail is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by VATSIM, Apple, or the other third-party services described below.

2. Information stored on your device

SimTrail stores certain information locally to provide its features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • App preferences, such as your home airport, map display choices, and interface settings.
  • Your saved flight history and flight tracks, including relevant flight details.
  • An unfinished flight track or other locally saved information needed to resume or complete a session.
  • Feedback drafts that you have not chosen to send.

Locally stored flight records and preferences are not automatically uploaded to a SimTrail-operated account or server. They remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them, or your device's own backup mechanisms include app data.

3. External data sources and network requests

SimTrail connects to third-party services to deliver its features. These connections may expose ordinary network information, such as your IP address, request time, and the requested resource, to the service provider. Depending on the feature, the app uses:

  • VATSIM: public virtual air traffic, pilot/controller information, flight plans, and ATC activity. Public VATSIM records can contain callsigns, network identifiers, names, and flight details.
  • Aviation Weather Center: METAR weather reports and flight-condition information for airports.
  • Apple Maps: map imagery and related mapping functionality.
  • Listen to VATSIM: optional ATC radio audio or related web content. When opened, requests are handled by that provider's website or streaming service.
  • OurAirports and other credited data sources: airport reference information and map-related data included with or used by the app.

SimTrail does not control the independent privacy practices or retention periods of those providers. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply.

4. Analytics, crash reports, and diagnostics

SimTrail may receive aggregated app analytics or crash reports through Apple's developer tools when Apple makes them available, subject to Apple's applicable privacy and diagnostic-sharing settings.

This version of SimTrail does not require a separate account for analytics. Apple handles its own diagnostics according to its policies. If additional third-party monitoring or analytics services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe them.

5. Feedback and support

If you choose to contact support, you may provide your email address, message, and any information you decide to include. Feedback drafts are kept on your device until you send them using your selected email or sharing app.

Information you send for support is used to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for these purposes and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the contact details below, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Advertising and tracking

SimTrail does not use your saved flight tracks for targeted advertising. The app does not require you to create a SimTrail advertising profile. Standard network requests to third-party providers are made to deliver the features you choose to use.

7. Data retention and deletion

Saved flights can be removed using the app's logbook controls. You can export a backup of your flight logbook from the app. Other local app data may be removed by deleting the app, subject to the operating system's backup behavior.

Deleting SimTrail does not automatically delete information held independently by VATSIM, Apple, or other providers. Please contact those providers regarding any data they independently control.

For support correspondence sent directly to the developer, contact us to request access or deletion where applicable.

8. Security

SimTrail uses platform security features to protect locally stored information and connects to external services over their supported secure network endpoints. No electronic system or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

SimTrail is intended as an aviation and flight-simulation utility. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to the developer through a support request, please contact us.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage local flight records, export a logbook backup, and choose whether to send feedback. You can also manage Apple's diagnostic-sharing preferences in your device settings.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data controlled by the developer. Send such requests to the email address below. Requests regarding information controlled by third-party providers should be directed to those providers.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SimTrail's features, integrations, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy requests, contact support@lindic-astrophotography.com.

SimTrail is a virtual aviation companion app. Information shown in the app is not intended for real-world flight planning, navigation, or operational decision-making.
