Frequently asked questions

Why can't I see an aircraft or controller?

SimTrail displays information received from VATSIM. Aircraft and controllers appear only when the relevant data is available. If an expected position or coverage area is missing, refresh the view and check the network connection. Coverage may not always be available for every online controller.

How often does live traffic update?

Aircraft positions depend on updates provided by the VATSIM network. The map is not a real-time surveillance radar, and displayed positions may update at intervals.

Why doesn't a radio station play?

Audio depends on station availability, network connectivity, and support for the selected controller. Not every online controller necessarily has a playable audio stream.

Does SimTrail show real-world commercial traffic?

No. SimTrail is designed around virtual flights and controllers connected to the VATSIM flight simulation network.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the Contact support button above to open a GitHub issue. Include the app version, device model, a description of the issue, and steps to reproduce it. Do not publish passwords, personal details or other sensitive information.
